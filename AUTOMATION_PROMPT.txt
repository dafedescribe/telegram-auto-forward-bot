- Two filters, applied in order:

  2. REMOTE (only when REMOTE_ONLY=1): keep messages matching regex \bremote\b|work\s*from\s*home|work-from-home|\bwfh\b|telecommut|\bhybrid\b (case-insensitive). Bare "virtual" must NOT count.
